## 26 March 2026

### PEACEPLUS Theme 5.2 Joint Project Launch

A joint launch event for the ACCLIMATISE, MOSAIC and C-MAP projects was held at AFBI Headquarters in Belfast. The three projects are funded through PEACEPLUS Theme 5.2 and administered by the Special EU Programmes Body (SEUPB).

The event brought together project partners, policymakers, stakeholders and representatives from across the marine and environmental sectors to mark the commencement of these important cross-border initiatives. Attendees had the opportunity to learn about the objectives of each project, explore opportunities for collaboration, and discuss the role of scientific research and innovation in supporting sustainable management of the shared marine environment.

## 15-25 July 2026

### ACCLIMATISE Research Cruise Completes Survey of the Irish and Malin Seas

Between 15 and 25 July 2026, seabird and marine mammal observers from the British Trust for Ornithology (BTO) and the Irish Whale and Dolphin Group (IWDG) joined scientists from the Agri-Food and Biosciences Institute (AFBI) aboard the RV *Corystes* for a multidisciplinary oceanographic research cruise in the Irish Sea and Malin Sea.

During the survey, the ACCLIMATISE team conducted dedicated seabird and marine mammal observations, deployed passive acoustic monitoring equipment to detect and record marine mammals, and collected plankton samples as part of a wider oceanographic sampling programme.

The data collected during the cruise will contribute to several key objectives of the ACCLIMATISE project, including the expansion of phytoplankton and zooplankton monitoring across Irish waters and improving understanding of the relationships between climate, oceanographic conditions and marine biodiversity. The survey also provided valuable opportunities for collaboration between project partners, helping to integrate observations of marine predators with information on the lower levels of the marine food web.

The research cruise represents an important step towards developing the evidence base needed to support sustainable, ecosystem-based management of the Irish and Malin Seas in a changing climate.
